Habitat / Behavior

Habitat

Stored textiles containing animal-derived fibers, including wool, fur, feathers and silk. Larvae feed on susceptible materials; adults do not feed. Draft pending verification.

Key Identification

Small golden-to-buff moth with narrow, largely unmarked wings. Larvae make silk webbing rather than portable cases. Draft pending verification.

Signs / IPM

Signs of Presence

Irregular fabric damage, webbing or silk tubes, larvae and cocoons in stored textiles. Draft pending verification.

IPM
